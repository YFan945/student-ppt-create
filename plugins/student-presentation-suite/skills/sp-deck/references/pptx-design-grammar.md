# Optional Composition Ideas

Follow [model-led-design.md](../../../references/model-led-design.md). These are ideas for expressing information, not required page grammars:

- A comparison can align evidence, use paired images, a table or annotated examples.
- A process can use a path, flow, swimlanes or a sequence of illustrated stages.
- A relationship can use a network, hierarchy, grouped objects or a concept map.
- A result can combine charts, observations, images and explanations.
- A quotation or key claim can use typography and supporting evidence.

Choose composition, emphasis, alignment, connectors, panels and decoration from the content and user references. Inspect rendered reading order, information relationships and readability; repair real obstruction or overflow. No fixed focal-share percentage, page-pattern quota or mandatory move is required.
