# Academic Rigorous（学术严谨）

- **Style character:** Formal, evidence-first and restrained; suited to teacher-scored academic work. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F8FAFC`; surface `FFFFFF`; primary text `0F172A`; secondary text `475569`; primary accent `1D4ED8`; secondary accent `8B9BAB`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `0D172D` with `C4D2E4` titles and `5C86F0` accents; content uses the light canvas `F8FAFC` with `FFFFFF` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `30447E` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `0D172D` / `192441` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F8FAFC` / `FFFFFF`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `academic-bracket` — Optional bracket or evidence-rail motif on cover, section, or one representative evidence page.
