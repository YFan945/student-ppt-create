# Charcoal Editorial（炭黑编辑）

- **Style character:** Editorial and ink-like; type, rules and crop marks carry the page ahead of colour. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F2F2F2`; surface `FFFFFF`; primary text `111111`; secondary text `4B5563`; primary accent `36454F`; secondary accent `C9B89B`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `1D1D1D` with `D6D6D6` titles and `7FA0B5` accents; content uses the light canvas `F2F2F2` with `FFFFFF` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `5C6B75` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `1D1D1D` / `2D2D2D` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F2F2F2` / `FFFFFF`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `editorial-crop` — Optional crop-mark motif for cover, section, or image-led editorial pages.
