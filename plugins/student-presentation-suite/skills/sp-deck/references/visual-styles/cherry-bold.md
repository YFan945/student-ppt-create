# Cherry Bold（樱桃醒目）

- **Style character:** Assertive and high-contrast; one decisive accent against a dark field. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `FCF6F5`; surface `FFFFFF`; primary text `182033`; secondary text `5B6475`; primary accent `990011`; secondary accent `2F3C7E`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `111829` with `E0C9C5` titles and `F04A5C` accents; content uses the light canvas `FCF6F5` with `FFFFFF` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `7E3038` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `111829` / `1D263D` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `FCF6F5` / `FFFFFF`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `cherry-slash` — Optional slash or directional motif for a decisive cover, comparison, or conclusion.
