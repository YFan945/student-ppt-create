# Midnight Business（午夜商务）

- **Style character:** Composed and corporate; a deep field with one confident accent. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F6F8FC`; surface `FFFFFF`; primary text `17213C`; secondary text `52627A`; primary accent `3157A4`; secondary accent `3B82F6`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `0E162C` with `C2CBDE` titles and `5A86E0` accents; content uses the light canvas `F6F8FC` with `FFFFFF` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `30497E` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `0E162C` / `192341` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F6F8FC` / `FFFFFF`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `midnight-beam` — Optional beam motif on cover, section, or closing; do not place it behind body copy.
