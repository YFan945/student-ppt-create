# Forest Moss（森林苔藓）

- **Style character:** Grounded and natural; muted green with an earthy surface. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F7F4E9`; surface `FFFCF4`; primary text `18351F`; secondary text `536454`; primary accent `2C5F2D`; secondary accent `97BC62`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `112A17` with `E7E3D3` titles and `55B357` accents; content uses the light canvas `F7F4E9` with `FFFCF4` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `3A733B` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `112A17` / `1C3D24` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F7F4E9` / `FFFCF4`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `moss-contour` — Optional contour motif on place, environment, section, or systems pages.
