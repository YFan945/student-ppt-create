# Warm Terracotta（暖陶土）

- **Style character:** Earthy and tactile; warm neutrals with a restrained clay accent. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F4EEDF`; surface `FFFDFC`; primary text `3D2C28`; secondary text `6F5B53`; primary accent `B85042`; secondary accent `A7BEAE`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `241916` with `DAD1BA` titles and `D4756A` accents; content uses the light canvas `F4EEDF` with `FFFDFC` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `7C3B32` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `241916` / `362723` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F4EEDF` / `FFFDFC`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `terracotta-stamp` — Optional stamp or archival motif on historical, evidence, or section pages.
