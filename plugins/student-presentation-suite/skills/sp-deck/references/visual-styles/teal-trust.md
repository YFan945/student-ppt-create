# Teal Trust（青绿可信）

- **Style character:** Calm and credible; institutional without feeling heavy. Hierarchy comes from a 40pt cover / 32pt title / 26pt statement / 22pt body / 11pt caption scale, and the accent only marks the emphasised line, the recommended option, the key number and the conclusion.
- **Palette:** canvas `F3FAF9`; surface `FFFFFF`; primary text `173B3F`; secondary text `536C70`; primary accent `028090`; secondary accent `00A896`.
- **Background reference:** cover uses the dark field `0E2A2D` with `D6E9E6` titles and `17B4C6` accents; content uses the light canvas `F3FAF9` with `FFFFFF` surfaces only where grouping is meaningful, plus an evidence-rail accent rule; section reuses the dark field with a single accent cue; closing returns to the dark field with a `30757E` hairline source rail. The dark scheme `0E2A2D` / `193D41` is the deliberate counterpart of the light `F3FAF9` / `FFFFFF`, so cover and body pages stay one colour family.
- **SVG reference:** `teal-checkpoint` — Optional checkpoint or handoff motif on service-flow and structural pages.
