# Cover composition and template inheritance

Read for a new/redesigned cover or a supplied template. Follow model-led-design.md for shared hierarchy and creative freedom.

## Cover

Choose from the title length, audience, identity information, topic imagery and user reference. The title is the reading focus; subtitle, author, course and date form a quieter, coherent group. Break long titles at semantic boundaries. Give text and image subjects usable space and contrast; do not let a logo, decoration or subtitle compete with the subject.

Typography, asymmetric image/text, full-image overlay or a relevant illustration may work. No fixed ratio, title size, centering or full-height panel is required. A simple cover does not make content pages sparse. Use original logos in proportion; fit evidence intact, crop decorative images only deliberately. Keep foreground titles editable, not baked into a background. An existing cover stays intact unless the edit calls for change.

If extra topic lists repeat the following content pages or compete with the title, merge them into a useful subtitle or move them into the body. Rich content does not require turning the cover into a second technical page; preserve additional cover detail when a user/reference actually calls for it.

Check the actual cover for reading order, title breaks, visual balance, text/image competition and grouped metadata, including at the intended viewing scale.

## Supplied reference

Distinguish strict template reuse, visual inspiration and content-only input. A deck supplied for content does not automatically dictate the design.

For strict reuse, inspect all relevant original slides before choosing layouts. Keep dimensions, masters, layouts, theme fonts, spacing, image treatment, navigation, headers, footers and required logos. Reuse source objects and useful placeholders on a copy; map the new material to fitting template layouts. Remove whole unused groups so empty icons/images do not remain. Do not cover the original with a newly drawn generic design.

For PDF/image references, rebuild separate editable text, shapes, lines and image objects; a full-slide screenshot is not editable reconstruction. Report material differences in delivery. Compare originals and final renders side by side, including slides affected by a shared master change. Required brands remain binding; factual integrity and usable readability must be resolved when they conflict with template treatment.
