# Content and presentation planning

Read the supplied material before deciding how to divide it into pages. Preserve the explanations, examples, evidence, methods, results and relationships needed to understand the subject. Organize them around the audience and the user's purpose; page order and content volume come from the material.

Before writing individual pages, choose and adapt an argument structure using `../skills/sp-outline/references/slide-structures.md`. This applies to planning inside sp-deck as well as outline-only work. Assign the main explanation of each claim/case to a page in the existing outline; distinguish overview, detailed explanation, evidence and synthesis. Check adjacent and non-adjacent pages for repeated explanations without new evidence or relationships. A rich deck needs information progression as well as rich individual pages. No extra outline approval or new handoff schema is required.

Do not reduce useful content to a slogan, keywords, isolated numbers or a generic three-part summary. Do not move explanations to speaker notes simply to produce a sparse slide. Notes support oral delivery; they do not replace information the audience needs to see.

Plan the complete visible content and the selective speaking path separately. A rich page may contain evidence, diagrams, examples and supporting explanations that the speaker does not read aloud. Highlight the spoken focus through weight, size, color and grouping; do not mechanically shrink visible content to match speaking time. Keep notes concise: the central point, the explanation needed to understand it and a meaningful transition. Full narration is only needed when explicitly requested.

## Plan substantive pages before drawing

对信息页先从材料组织充分的正文：主题/发现、相关解释、事实或案例支撑、需要看见的关系，以及有依据的含义。按页功能取舍组合；不要把每组内容压成“名称＋一句用途”，也不要把保留事实理解成只留下数字。将已有方法细节、对象、条件、对比维度、具体过程、结果解释和有来源的案例纳入可浏览的正文，形成足够支撑重点的内容组。例子与图解可解释既有材料，不能新增未提供的调研、成果、因果或数值。

在已有handoff-plan/Slide Spec的content与visual中，先记录各页实际展示的内容组、主要视觉及其来源/数据/生成或占位方案，再编写页面。不要仅写“做一个流程图/放图标”，应明确图像要展示什么、图表回答什么、哪些解释与之对应。检查整套是否有丰富正文而非多页重述同一主张。讲稿另选重点；大段剩余空白、重复口号、加大的标题和小图标不能替代支撑内容。

材料不足时先检索已有用户文件、梳理可解释的细节和关系；允许外查时补证据，允许生图/占位时补主要视觉。来源确实不足则在intake明确具体缺口与适合的篇幅，不靠增加空页或把免责声明写上屏解决。封面、转场和结束页按各自功能集中构图，不套用信息页的内容量。

Preserve useful detail without duplicating claims or inventing filler. Different page functions may have different densities. Put optional supplementary material in a suitable page or appendix when requested or allowed; do not hide necessary visible explanation in notes. Explicit oral requirements remain in the speaking plan even when most slide detail is covered selectively.

Editorial expansion must preserve the strength and scope of the material: “record original, source and version separately” does not imply “never store them together”; retaining raw boundaries/indexes does not establish a blanket guarantee that every representation is unchanged. Label interpretations as such when needed and avoid adding unsupported prohibitions or guarantees to fill a rich page.

Labels must match depicted categories: describe documents as materials and cameras/recorders as devices. A familiar icon arrangement does not supply evidence for a new object category or process relationship.

For sparse materials, identify the actual missing information and inspect the supplied files or request it. Expand only with supported explanations, worked examples, meaningful relationships or authorized evidence. Do not invent outcomes, quotes, photographs or numbers to fill a page.

A Slide Spec records the intended information and exact facts. It does not prescribe a per-page claim quota, narrative template, title zone or closing band. Use shared-standards.md for audience-facing language and model-led-design.md for production.

Question titles also contain assumptions: “不知道科研入口” does not establish that all lower-year students have not begun research. Preserve “部分” rather than rewriting it as “很多/全部”; do not invent willingness, actual registration records or observed outcomes. Explanatory purpose may clarify the supplied plan, but cannot silently create a new fact.
