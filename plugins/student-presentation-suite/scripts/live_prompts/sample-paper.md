Title: Lightweight Retrieval-Augmented Generation for On-Device Tutors
Authors: Y. An, et al.
Year: 2025

## Abstract
We propose a 120M-parameter retrieval-augmented generation model that runs
entirely on-device, targeting low-resource tutoring scenarios.

## Key results
- Accuracy on the TutorQA benchmark: 78.4%
- Inference latency: 220 ms on a Snapdragon 8 Gen 3
- Training data: 1.2M QA pairs
- Compared to a 7B baseline, our model reaches 96% of its accuracy at 1/58th the size.

## Related work
Prior on-device tutors rely on cloud APIs, incurring latency and privacy cost.
